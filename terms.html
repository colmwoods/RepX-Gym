Terms and Conditions

  1. Membership Agreement: By signing up, you agree to abide by all RepX Gym rules and policies.
  2. Minimum Age: Members must be at least 16 years old. Those under 18 require parental consent.
  3. Membership Fees: All fees are payable in advance. Missed payments may result in suspended access.
  4. Cancellations: Monthly memberships can be canceled anytime with 7 days' notice before the next billing cycle.
  5. Refunds: Membership fees are non-refundable except in cases of documented medical issues.
  6. Access: Your membership grants you access during staffed hours unless otherwise stated.
  7. Code of Conduct: Members must respect staff, equipment, and fellow gym-goers at all times.
  8. Health & Safety: Members must ensure they are medically fit to exercise. RepX Gym is not liable for injuries.
  9. Personal Belongings: RepX Gym is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
  10. Changes to Terms: RepX Gym reserves the right to amend these terms with appropriate notice.
  11. Equipment Usage: Members must use equipment properly and return weights and accessories to their designated places after use.
  12. Dress Code: Appropriate workout attire and clean athletic footwear are required at all times for hygiene and safety.
  13. Guest Policy: Guests must be signed in, pay applicable fees, and adhere to all gym rules. Members are responsible for their guests' conduct.
  14. Locker Usage: Lockers are for daily use only unless otherwise arranged. Items left overnight may be removed.
  15. Class Reservations: Group classes may require advance booking. Missed bookings without cancellation may result in limited future reservations.
  16. Behavior Policy: Aggressive, inappropriate, or disruptive behavior will result in warnings or immediate termination of membership.
  17. Photos & Videos: Recording or photography inside the gym requires prior approval to respect others’ privacy.
  18. Smoking & Substance Use: Smoking, alcohol, and drug use are strictly prohibited on gym premises.
  19. Pet Policy: No pets are allowed in the gym, with the exception of certified service animals.
  20. Emergency Closures: In case of emergencies or required maintenance, RepX Gym may close temporarily without prior notice, with reasonable effort made to inform members.
