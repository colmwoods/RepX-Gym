Equipment

Explore our range of gym equipment available for use at RepX Gym.

🏋️ Dumbbells

A versatile staple in any strength training routine, dumbbells allow for a wide range of exercises targeting every major muscle group. Perfect for free weight training, stability work, and muscle isolation.

🚣 Rowing Machine

Combining cardio and strength, the rowing machine offers a full-body workout that mimics the motion of rowing on water. It’s low-impact and ideal for improving endurance and burning calories.

🚴 Bike Machine

Great for cardiovascular fitness, the stationary bike provides a smooth, joint-friendly workout. Adjustable resistance levels make it suitable for all fitness levels, from warm-ups to intense rides.

🏋️‍♂️ Chest Press Machine

Target your upper body with controlled resistance to build strength in the chest, shoulders, and triceps. The chest press machine ensures proper form and safety, especially for beginners.

⛷️ SkiErg Machine

This innovative cardio machine simulates the movement of Nordic skiing, offering an intense upper-body and core workout. It’s perfect for improving power, endurance, and coordination.

🛠️ Smith Machine

A guided barbell system that enhances safety and stability during strength training. Ideal for squats, presses, and lunges — especially for those lifting heavy without a spotter.

🦵 Leg Press Machine

Effectively targets quads, hamstrings, and glutes with controlled resistance. The leg press machine is ideal for building lower-body strength while reducing stress on joints.

🏃 Treadmill

A go-to cardio machine for walking, jogging, or running. With adjustable speed and incline settings, the treadmill suits all fitness levels and helps improve cardiovascular endurance.
