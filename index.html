About Us

At RepX Gym, we believe fitness is more than just reps and sets — it’s a lifestyle. Founded on the idea that everyone deserves a space to grow stronger, feel confident, and push limits, we’re here to help you become your best self — physically and mentally. Whether you're lifting for the first time, hitting new PRs, or just looking for a supportive space to sweat it out, RepX has your back. Our facility combines state-of-the-art equipment, dynamic classes, and a welcoming community that moves together — rep by rep. From personal training and HIIT to strength zones and recovery spaces, RepX is built to inspire. 💪 Join the movement.
